# Regulated Decisions Compliance Matrix & Statutory Standards

## 1. CMS-0057-F: Interoperability and Prior Authorization Final Rule

### Regulatory Mandate
Promulgated by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), CMS-0057-F establishes sweeping federal requirements for Medicare Advantage, Medicaid, and CHIP health plans regarding Prior Authorization:
1. **Specific Denial Rationales:** Plans are strictly prohibited from issuing generic denial letters. Every denial must state the exact clinical reason, cite specific policy criteria that were not satisfied, and reference the underlying clinical record.
2. **Standardized Turnaround Times:**
   - Urgent / Expedited Prior Authorization requests must be adjudicated within **72 hours**.
   - Standard Prior Authorization requests must be adjudicated within **7 calendar days** (or state-mandated 72h-14d windows).
3. **Public Metrics Reporting:** Plans must publish annual prior authorization approval and denial rates, automated decision percentages, and appeal overturn rates.

## 2. ERISA Section 503 & Claims Procedure Regulations (29 CFR 2560.503-1)

### Regulatory Mandate
Governing employer-sponsored commercial health benefit plans, ERISA § 503 guarantees a "full and fair review":
1. **Notice of Adverse Benefit Determination:** Must set forth the specific reason or reasons for the denial and reference the specific plan provisions or clinical protocols on which the determination is based.
2. **Access to Clinical Guidelines:** If an internal rule, guideline, protocol, or similar criterion was relied upon, the plan must either provide a copy free of charge or state that such rule was relied upon and will be provided upon request.
3. **Appeals Rights:** Members must be granted a minimum of **180 calendar days** following receipt of an adverse benefit determination to appeal.
4. **Physician Reviewer Independence:** Appeals of determinations based on medical necessity must be conducted by a healthcare professional who was neither consulted in the initial determination nor the subordinate of any individual involved.

## 3. CMS Two-Midnight Rule (CMS-1599-F; 42 CFR § 412.3)

### Regulatory Mandate
Defines medical necessity criteria for Medicare Part A Acute Inpatient hospital admissions versus Part B Outpatient Observation stays:
- An inpatient admission is considered medically necessary if the admitting physician expects the beneficiary to require hospital care that will span across at least **two midnights**, and that expectation is documented in the medical record.
- Uncomplicated evaluations (e.g. short-stay chest pain rule-outs with negative cardiac biomarkers and normal ECG) that resolve in under 24 hours must be classified as **Outpatient Observation**, not Inpatient Part A.

## 4. CMS Claims Processing Manual Ch. 12 § 30.6.6 & OIG Modifier 25 Guidelines

### Regulatory Mandate
Modifier 25 allows payment for an Evaluation and Management (E/M) service on the same day as a minor procedure (0 or 10-day global period):
- The documentation must substantiate a **significant, separately identifiable** medical service that required cognitive work distinctly above and beyond the normal pre-procedure, intra-procedure, and post-procedure care inherent in the procedure itself.
- Billing Modifier 25 for routine procedure consent, site preparation, or dressing instructions is classified as **unbundled billing** and subject to False Claims Act (FCA) and OIG audit penalties.

### Veridict Implementation
- Policy `POL-CLAIMS-MOD25-2026` audits E/M notes paired with minor procedures (e.g. CPT 20610 joint injection).
